Returns & refunds

How to return an item and when you get your money back.

Return window

Unopened items in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery.

Items we cannot accept

Opened food, treats and litter cannot be returned unless the product is faulty or has passed its expiry date. Prescription items and personalised products are non-returnable.

How to return

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and a photo of the item. We'll arrange a pickup from the same address, usually the next day.

Refunds

KNET and card refunds are returned to the original card within 7 to 10 working days. Cash orders are refunded as store credit or by bank transfer.
